# CollabX

CollabX is a platform for finding the right people to build with. Whether you're starting a hackathon project, launching a startup, or diving into research, finding team members who actually complement your skills is hard. This platform makes that easier.

## What it does

You create a profile with your skills and interests. Then you can either post a project you want to work on or browse projects others have created. If you find a project that looks interesting, you apply to join. The system looks at skill overlap and helps surface people who would actually work well together instead of just letting friends default into teams.

Once you're on a team, there's a built-in chat so you can communicate in real time. You can also use invite codes to bring specific people into your project without the application process.

## The core features

- Create projects with a category (hackathon, startup, research), required skills, and team size
- Browse projects and apply to ones you want to join
- Profiles that show your skills, interests, and a bit about you
- Real-time messaging with teammates
- Invite codes for quick team assembly
- A skill-based matching system that considers who would work well together

## Who it's for

Anyone who wants to build something with other people but doesn't want to stick with the same group every time. Hackathon participants. Side project founders. Students looking for research collaborators. People just looking to work on interesting things with capable people.

## Why it exists

Most team-finding happens through personal networks or by default (your friends). That limits who you meet and who gets to participate in projects. CollabX takes the friction out of finding new collaborators based on what they can actually do, not just who you already know.
